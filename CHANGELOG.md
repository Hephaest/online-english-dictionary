# Online English Dictionary Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search a word in Cambridge, Longman, Oxford Learner's, Merriam-Webster, or Urban Dictionary from one window, and switch between them with ⌘] and ⌘[.
- Jump straight to a word's entry from the text selected in any app with Define Selected Word.
- Hear the US pronunciation with Enter and the UK pronunciation with ⌘ Enter.
- See every meaning grouped by role (noun, verb, and so on) on the left, with the full definition, examples, learner badges, and the picture on the right.
- Open the whole entry at reading width with ⌘⇧ Enter.
- Find your recent words under the search bar and remove any of them with ⌃X.
